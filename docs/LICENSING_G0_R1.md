For this 2026-10-10 publication the project author explicitly authorized all LAD project code and research data to be published in the named repository and its Release. Earlier snapshot documents stating synthetic-only, private or withheld describe the older publication boundary and are not exclusions under this new authorization.

No new blanket MIT, CC0 or other software/data license is invented. Existing file attribution, rights statements and third-party notices remain. The original project has no unified top-level software license; publication access alone does not grant rights beyond applicable law or the files' existing terms. Contact the rightsholder for additional reuse permission where required.

Unlike the earlier narrow snapshot, this release includes authorized course-text sources, anonymized learning records, saved research answers, evaluation materials and the actual latest thesis/supplement. Third-party libraries, model dependency caches, full pretrained MPNet weights and fonts are not vendored. The optional model is obtained from its publisher at the exact configured revision; its existing model terms apply. Small author research heads and arrays are separately inventoried in the research asset.

Original anonymous rater IDs and scores are preserved. Signed/identity-only paperwork, credentials and authentication material are excluded. See PROJECT_MAP.md and RECOVERY.md for exact publication scope and transformations, and retained research/LICENSING.md and research/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for the earlier snapshot and method attribution. Those historical files are retained rather than rewritten as current publication statements.
