# Data availability and publication scope

The public snapshot contains selected saved numerical materials that the author authorized for this release. Source documents and old research archives are not themselves publication allowlists.

| Data layer | Public contents and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| RQ1 | Rank-frequency counts and fixed-label confusion matrices from frozen evaluation outputs. Sufficient for marginal metrics; no original utterances, learner records, per-query rankings or reconstructed paired outcomes. |
| RQ2 and R7 | Anonymous task/family identifiers, requirement-bit Gold and saved predicted bits, plus frozen numeric configuration. Supports core32/full36 metric recomputation and the saved serializer comparison. Original contextual inputs and private labels-to-person mappings are excluded. |
| RQ3 and S7 | Anonymous criterion scores/NA and fixed applicability/condition/family metadata, neutral fixed-rater columns, numerical reference tables and the frozen statistical specification. These support numerical aggregation; no answer text, private histories, rater identities or declarations are included. |
| Synthetic fixtures | Explicitly labelled artificial module examples for code contracts. They do not replace withheld experimental inputs and are not evidence of study performance. |
| External inputs | Original student/dashboard records, private history, complete course material and third-party weights are not distributed. Access or redistribution rights have not been established by this release. No automatic access-on-request promise is made. |

All units, identifiers, missing-value semantics and exact starting levels are described in the component documents and schemas. NA retains frozen applicability; poor scores are not converted to NA. No historical answers, scores, failures or negative families were regenerated or removed for performance reasons. No new human scoring was performed.

For a full rerun, supply the documented schema-compatible inputs through explicit paths after obtaining the necessary rights. The code snapshot does not provide an authorization route for private student or third-party data. No DOI or external data-deposit identifier has been minted. The fixed GitHub tag and source-file hashes identify this release; it is not a claim of complete archival persistence or fully licensed FAIR reuse.

Licence boundaries are in LICENSING.md. H9/D9 active work and unreturned supplements are not part of v1.0.0.
