PRIMARY CRITERIA AND ANCHORS

Score each applicable criterion independently.

CLARITY — clear, understandable, and free of unnecessary jargon.
1: substantially unintelligible, disorganised, or unrecoverably jargon-heavy.
2: partly recoverable but difficult to follow because of repeated ambiguity, structure, or unexplained terms.
3: broadly understandable with a material local clarity or organisation weakness.
4: clear and well organised with only a minor wording or structure issue.
5: consistently clear, concise, logically organised, and easy for the student to understand.

PERSONALISATION — appropriate use of student-specific data, context, preferences, constraints, or prior information.
1: invents, contradicts, or materially misuses personal information, or ignores essential available personal context.
2: superficial or poorly targeted use with a major relevant detail missed.
3: some correct tailoring, but shallow or materially incomplete.
4: appropriate use of nearly all material personal context, with a minor missed opportunity.
5: precise, proportionate tailoring to all material available personal context without invented traits.

ACTIONABILITY — specific, feasible strategies or next steps where appropriate.
1: no usable step when required, or an infeasible, unsafe, or evidence-incompatible action.
2: vague advice lacking necessary specificity, feasibility, or sequence.
3: at least one feasible step, with a material specificity, prioritisation, sequencing, or fit weakness.
4: specific, feasible, evidence-linked next steps with only a minor omission.
5: concrete, feasible, prioritised, well-sequenced steps adapted to constraints, with a proportionate progress check.

CONTEXT_AWARENESS — correct integration of relevant Dashboard, conversation, course, and learner context.
1: ignores or contradicts material context, confuses scope, or answers as though different context applied.
2: uses only a small part and materially misinterprets or omits another needed source.
3: uses core context correctly but incompletely synthesises available sources.
4: correctly integrates nearly all relevant context and boundaries, with a minor omission.
5: accurately and coherently integrates every material source and distinguishes their scopes.

MOTIVATION_AND_ENGAGEMENT — supportive tone encouraging engagement without manipulation or empty praise.
1: shaming, dismissive, manipulative, coercive, or materially displaced by empty praise.
2: discouraging, detached, patronising, or overpraising and poorly matched to the situation.
3: acceptable and somewhat supportive, but generic, uneven, or weakly engaging.
4: appropriately supportive, honest, and autonomy-respecting, with a minor missed opportunity.
5: calibrated, credible, autonomy-supportive encouragement without manipulation or empty praise.

RELIABILITY — alignment with the student's evidence without misleading or fabricated claims.
1: a material fabricated, false, seriously misleading, or unsafe claim.
2: multiple substantive unsupported claims or a major overstatement despite some correct content.
3: broadly aligned, with one material imprecision, weak inference, or inadequate uncertainty statement.
4: material claims are accurate and supported or qualified, with only a minor non-misleading imprecision.
5: every material claim is accurately scoped; uncertainty and limits are explicit; nothing misleading or fabricated appears.

MULTI_PAGE_COHESIVENESS — coherence when evidence concerns multiple LAD pages.
1: materially contradicts, conflates, or misaggregates evidence across pages.
2: fragmented page treatment or a materially misleading cross-page relationship.
3: broadly coherent with one material relationship missed or a noticeable tension.
4: coherent integration that distinguishes page evidence, with a minor omission or transition weakness.
5: full synthesis across all material pages while distinguishing metrics, time windows, and scopes.

ERROR_HANDLING — safe handling of missing, incomplete, conflicting, ambiguous, or unavailable information without guessing.
1: guesses, fabricates, hides, or recklessly resolves a material problem or boundary.
2: partly notices the problem but makes a consequential assumption or misses essential clarification.
3: identifies the main problem without major fabrication, but incompletely clarifies, limits, safely answers, or recovers.
4: clearly states uncertainty or boundary, avoids guessing, and gives an appropriate clarification or next step with a minor omission.
5: precisely distinguishes the relevant problem, safely answers what is supported, and requests only the focused clarification or recovery action needed.
